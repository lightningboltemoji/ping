# The icon holds up in every appearance

> Change `1792908142` — 2026-10-24

## Goal

The icon worked in one of macOS 26's six icon appearances. In Clear and Tinted the black bell came back
as a faint emboss, close to invisible, because the system builds those from a layer's luminance. In
Dark the icon stayed a bright lime square, since the dark fill had been set to the light one so the
system would not darken the ground under a black bell. `IMPLEMENTATION.md §14` called both the price
of the black bell. The plate's gradient also ran from saturated lime to a washed-out pale at the
bottom, and `Icon/` carried 2.5 MB of original flat artwork that nothing reads.

## Implementation

**The bell is recoloured per appearance, not changed.** A layer takes `fill-specializations` just as
the icon's background does, keyed `dark` and `tinted`. `tinted` covers Clear as well; there is no
separate key for it. The bell stays black in Regular, goes white under `tinted` and takes the plate's
lime in Dark. The layer has no default entry, so Regular draws the SVG's own black and `bell.svg` stays
a drawing that shows something on its own. Rejected: a white bell everywhere, which fixes Clear and
Tinted without specializations but needs a dark plate to hold contrast, and so a different icon.

**Dark inverts rather than darkens.** The plate becomes `system-dark` and the bell the lime, so the
icon is the same two colours swapped instead of a lime square in a dark Dock.

**The plate is one colour, `#7CFC00`, as an `automatic-gradient`.** Chosen from eight flat limes
between `#39FF14` and `#CCFF00`, all 14–18:1 against black, each rendered both solid and automatic.
The automatic gradient is close to invisible but not nothing: in Icon Composer's render the plate goes
from `#A7F55D` at the top to `#9AF153` at the bottom, where solid stays even. The old two-stop gradient
went from `#94F360` to `#BBF598`, lighter at the bottom. Glass lightens the plate, so `#7CFC00` renders
near `#A9F65E`; the hex is the input, not what is seen.

**`icon.json` is in Icon Composer's serialisation:** Foundation's pretty printer with sorted keys,
`"key" : value`. A save from Icon Composer is then a minimal diff, not every line. Checked by
round-tripping a checked-in Icon Composer file through the same printer: the only differences were
floats like `0.1`, which Foundation prints at full precision, and this file has none.

**`Icon/Ping.png` and `Icon/Ping.pxd` are deleted.** They are the flat full-bleed original that
`bell.svg` was traced from, and only `Ping.icon` is built.

**Verified through what ships.** `make app` compiled the icon with no actool notices or warnings.
`NSWorkspace.icon(forFile:)` on fresh copies of the bundle, with each of the six
`AppleIconAppearanceTheme` tokens as a process argument, gave black on lime in Regular Light, lime on
near-black in Regular Dark, and a solid bell in Clear Light, Clear Dark, Tinted Light and Tinted Dark.

## Observations

- Tinted Dark draws the bell mid-grey, not white. That is the system's ramp for that appearance; the
  fill under `tinted` is white either way.
- Not seen in Finder or System Settings at each Icon & widget style, only through `NSWorkspace`.
  `LSUIElement` means there is no Dock tile to look at.
- Not opened and saved in Icon Composer, so the round trip is inferred from the serialisation, not done.
- The glass has little to light on one flat black layer. Splitting the bell and its clapper into two
  groups would give each its own shadow and highlight. Left alone, because the flat pixel bell is the
  look.
